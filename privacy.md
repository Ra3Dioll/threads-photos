# Privacy Policy — Threads assistant for @alexey_partner

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Threads account @alexey_partner
to manage his own account. It is not offered to other users.

**What data the app accesses:** the owner's Threads profile, his own posts, insights
(views, likes, replies), replies and mentions on his posts, and public Threads posts
returned by keyword search or public profile lookup.

**How data is used:** to publish the owner's scheduled posts, show him statistics,
remind him about unanswered replies and suggest post ideas. Data is processed on the
owner's private server and is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

**Storage:** an access token and short reports are stored on the owner's private server.
Public posts from search are used only for analysis and are not republished.

**Data deletion:** see [data deletion instructions](data-deletion.md). Contact: Telegram @Ra3Diol.
